Detective Conan Trading Card Game

Conan TCG Primer

Learn the rules in the order you will meet them at the table, then try them on small tools and a quiz. You play a detective, gather evidence faster than your opponent, and solve the case first.

Source: Official Rule Manual (Japanese PDF, Takara Tomy)

Win conditionHave at least as many evidence cards as your case's level, with the case in its solution phase, then declare Case Solved.
How you get evidenceDeduce with characters or your partner, or take Actions against the opponent's case.
How you lose groundYour characters get removed in Contact, your evidence gets taken, and an empty deck hands your opponent evidence.

Manual FILE.1 · Card types

Four kinds of card

Partner

Your sidekick. Every partner shares the same two abilities, Assist and Case Solved, and can also deduce. Its LP tells you how much evidence a deduction earns.

Character

Enters Scene to deduce and take Actions. Has a level, AP, LP, a color (some have two), a trait and abilities.

Event

A one-shot effect. Played from hand, resolved, then placed in the Remove area.

Case

The case you must solve. Its level is the evidence you need, and it differs for first and second player. It starts in its case phase and later flips to its solution phase.

Reading a character card

Level
How many cards you need in your FILE area to play it from hand.
AP
Action Point. Compared against the other character in a Contact.
LP
Logic Point, shown by a keyhole. The amount of evidence you gain when this character deduces.
Color
Limits when you can play it. A two-color character counts as either color.
ID
Decides whether two cards are "the same card".
Level, AP and LP have no lower limit. A character at 0 or below is not removed because of that.

Same card, different art

Parallel versions and reprints with a matching ID count as the same card.

  • All versions together count toward the limit of 3 copies in a deck.
  • If a card is missing some printed information because of its design, it still has the information of the same card.
  • A corrected card text applies to every version.

Deducing, taking Actions, guarding, and abilities that need sleep turn a card sideways. A stunned card is upside down, and when it would be activated it becomes sleeping instead. Sleeping or stunning a card that is already stunned changes nothing.

Manual FILE.2 · Flow of the game

Setup and deck rules

Build the deck

  • Exactly 40 cards, characters and events only.
  • At most 3 of the same card (same ID).
  • Any colors may go in the deck. Colors limit what you can play during the game.
  • Besides the deck you need one partner and one case, 42 cards in total.

Before turn one

  1. Place your case and partner face down.
  2. Shuffle your deck and place it.
  3. Decide first player at random, for example rock-paper-scissors. The winner goes first.
  4. Draw 5 cards.
  5. You may redraw once: put any number back, shuffle, draw that many. The first player decides first.
  6. Flip your case and partner face up and begin.
Manual FILE.2 · Turn flow

One turn, three phases

Players alternate turns. Each turn runs Auto, Main, then End. Step through it below.

    Can I play this card? Level check

    You can play a card whose level is no higher than the number of cards in your FILE area. Your partner counts while it sits in the FILE area after an Assist.

    Manual FILE.2 · Contact

    Contact: comparing AP

    A Contact happens when an Action targets a character that was not guarded, or when a guard is declared. The attacker's AP is compared with the other character's AP. The lower-AP player gets to act first. The attacker is never removed.

    vs
    Cut-in with AP +2000 is the manual's own example. Real cards differ, so read the card. Each player may act once per Contact, with a Cut-in or a Disguise, or pass.
    Manual FILE.3 · Terms

    Glossary

    English labels are my own translations. Japanese terms are shown so you can match them to your cards.

    Refresh

    When your deck runs out, even mid-action, shuffle your Remove area into a new deck and your opponent gains 1 evidence. If your Remove area is empty when you must refresh, you lose.

    Effects that draw, gain evidence, or place cards from the deck finish the remaining count after the refresh. Effects that only look at or reveal cards use what is left and do not refresh.

    MR cards

    From expansion pack 5. An MR enters Scene as a character. Only one MR can exist across your Scene and partner area, so a new MR removes the old one.

    If an MR leaves Scene during your opponent's turn, it moves to your partner area. There it cannot deduce or act, but its abilities still work.

    Names with several characters

    A card named with & or parentheses, such as a double name, counts as every name in it, in every area. Bond and similar checks treat it as each of those names.
