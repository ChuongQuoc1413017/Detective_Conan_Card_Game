Detective School 🔍

Five short courses in thinking like Conan: Become a detective (from curiosity and observation to collecting clues and reasoning), Body language (reading faces and gestures, kindly), Staying safe (what to do when something seems suspicious or dangerous), Become a polymath (learning skills, remembering more, keeping body and mind healthy) and Be creative (finding the solution no one else imagined). Every lesson has a small activity to try right away, and each section has its own certificate!

👪 Made for kids and young adults. Every case here is a friendly one: lost pets, borrowed things and secret messages. Real detectives stay safe: ask permission, respect privacy, and go to a trusted adult when you need help.
