Play a practice game

Pick one of six starter decks, or the deck you built on the Deck page, and play a full game against a bot. Pick its level (Novice, Sleuth or Master Sleuth) and its deck, or leave the deck random. The page greys out moves you cannot make and tells you why. Tap a character in Scene to read its ability.

With your own deck: only abilities the game fully understands work. A card marked ⚠️ has text that does nothing. Disguise, cards set on characters, face-up evidence, Investigate and Case abilities are partly supported. MR cards follow their special rules: an MR leaving the Scene during the opponent's turn moves to the Partner area, a new MR removes the old one, and abilities marked for the Partner area still work there.

ℹ️ About these decks and what is simplified
Decks: CT-D01 Conan (blue), CT-D07 Black Organization (black), CT-D02 Heiji Hattori (green) and CT-D03 Kid the Phantom Thief (white). CT-D04 Akai (red) and CT-D05 Amuro (yellow). Ability text is paraphrased. Simplified: no Disguise (none of these decks use it), cards put on the bottom of the deck are not ordered, and the bot does not plan several turns ahead. Check the official manual for exact wording.
