Decks: CT-D01 Conan (blue), CT-D07 Black Organization (black), CT-D02 Heiji Hattori (green) and CT-D03 Kid the Phantom Thief (white). CT-D04 Akai (red) and CT-D05 Amuro (yellow). Ability text is paraphrased. Simplified: no Disguise (none of these decks use it), cards put on the bottom of the deck are not ordered, and the bot is simple.
